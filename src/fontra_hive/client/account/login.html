Fontra Hive is a paid service on top of Fontra, for type teams, from €9 per designer per month, reviewers free. Try it free for 21 days: during the beta, access is by invitation.

What Hive adds to Fontra

The full Fontra editor in your browser, nothing to install, with a shared home for your team's fonts.

Every change is kept

Each edit is saved as a version, signed by who made it. Open a glyph's history, hover any version to see it drawn over the current one, click to keep it on the canvas, and restore it in one step. Name a state of the whole font with a snapshot: the changes since the previous one are grouped under it.

Draw together

Everyone works on the same font at the same time and sees who is there. Share a project with a role: observer, reviewer, designer, manager or admin. Observers and reviewers see without changing anything.

Bring your fonts

Start a project from any format Fontra reads: a .designspace with its UFOs, .ufo, .glyphs, .glyphspackage, .fontra, or a compiled .ttf, .otf, .woff2. Import again later: it becomes a new version, the history stays.

Take them anywhere

Download the sources as a .fontra package or a .designspace with its UFOs, or fonts built on the server, the way Fontra Pak builds them: .otf, .ttf and .woff2.

  • Teams and organizations. Owners, members, a role for every project, and an option to keep an organization's projects to its members.
  • Stored as git. Every project is a complete .fontra package in its own git repository: the whole history, nothing locked in.
  • Hosted in Europe. Servers in Paris, a backup every night in another region.
  • Built on Fontra. The open-source, browser-based font editor, used as it is released.
